Utah, U.S.A.

Utah is known as the beehive state. It is a mountainous region that used to be a desert, but has blossomed through excellent irrigation. At one point, it was part of Mexico, but now is part of the U.S. In the spring, you can see both snow-capped mountains and lush greenery.
